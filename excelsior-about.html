Company Profile

A Trusted Name In Bangladesh Courier Service

Excelsior Express has been delivering for businesses and individuals across Bangladesh since 1986. It is one of the largest independent express delivery companies in the country.

We handle documents and parcels for corporates, SMEs, online merchants, the RMG sector and individuals. Customers can choose same-day, next-day or express delivery, with competitive pricing and real-time tracking on every shipment.

Development History

1986

Founded In Dhaka. Excelsior Express starts with one promise: take every parcel from concept to completion.

2012

Coverage Extended. Delivery service expands to most areas of Bangladesh.

2014

Air Parcel Launched. Air shipment service starts for customers who need quick, on-demand delivery.

2019

Technology-Enabled Service. Technology infrastructure upgraded to meet the needs of modern courier service.

Today

23 Service Points. Branches in Dhaka, Chattogram, Narayanganj, Cumilla and Rangpur, including 5 EPZ desks.
